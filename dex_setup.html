Official Google Chrome on Android & Samsung DeX

Crop & Stream on Samsung Galaxy Fold 3

Because official Chrome for Android does not support extensions, use either of these two zero-install solutions built specifically for Samsung DeX.

⭐ Method 1: The "Focus Crop" Bookmarklet (Recommended)

Works right inside official Google Chrome on any website (Semrush, Divya Aarogyam, etc.). Isolates your dashboard into a clean, fullscreen view without any sidebar or URL bar, ready for Google Meet.

  1. Tap the button below to copy the Bookmarklet code.
  2. In Google Chrome on Samsung DeX, bookmark any page (tap the star ⭐ icon).
  3. Edit the bookmark:
    • Name: 🎯 Region Crop
    • URL: Paste the copied code.
  4. Now open your dashboard (e.g. Semrush). Type Region Crop in the address bar and tap your bookmark!
  5. Tap the dashboard: it instantly expands to fullscreen, hiding all sidebars, tabs, and URL bars!
💡 In Google Meet: Share your Chrome screen/window. Since the dashboard is in fullscreen mode, viewers in Meet will see only your clean dashboard without any tabs or sidebars.
