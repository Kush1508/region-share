Share Cropped Tab on Samsung DeX

Works in official Google Chrome on Samsung Galaxy Fold 3 / Samsung DeX without needing any extension installed.

1. Tap Select Tab / Screen.
2. Drag the cyan box to crop only your dashboard.
3. Tap Pop into Floating Window (PiP) to get a floating borderless window for Google Meet.
